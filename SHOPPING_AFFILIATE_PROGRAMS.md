# Shopping Sites: Affiliate Program Check

The Shopping page (`frontend/src/pages/public/Shopping.jsx`) lists 122 sites. I checked each one online on **21 Nov 2026** to see whether it pays affiliate commission.

## Summary

| Result | Sites |
|---|---|
| ✅ Has an affiliate program | **108** |
| ⚠️ Doubtful (paused, click-only, or the match is unclear) | **7** |
| ❌ No program found | **7** |
| **Total** | **122** |

- **Fewer programs than sites.** Some sites share their parent's program: Amazon Books with Amazon India, Flipkart Books with Flipkart, JioMart Groceries with JioMart, and BabyHug with FirstCry. That leaves about **104 separate programs**.
- **The easiest way in is a network.** Joining **INRDeals** and **Cuelinks** covers about 100 of these sites between them, and the two networks are listed against each site below. EarnKaro, vCommission and Admitad also carry many of the same brands.
- **Own direct programs (checked):**
  - Amazon Associates India (affiliate-program.amazon.in)
  - Nykaa Affiliate Program (affiliate.nykaa.com, a flat 8% on every category)
  - IndiaMart (affiliate.indiamart.com)
  - Flipkart (affiliate.flipkart.com). Several sources say Flipkart pauses new sign-ups from time to time, but its campaigns are live on both networks.
- **Own direct programs (not checked here):** AliExpress Portals, eBay Partner Network and iHerb Rewards are also known to run their own global programs. Whether Indian publishers can join eBay's is unclear.

## How this was checked

1. For each site I fetched its campaign page on **Cuelinks** (`cuelinks.com/campaigns/<store>-affiliate-program`) and **INRDeals** (`inrdeals.com/campaigns/<store>-affiliate-program`).
   - Both networks show the current payout in the page title, and that is the figure recorded below.
   - A Cuelinks page marked **"paused by the advertiser"** is flagged as *paused*.
   - INRDeals shows every campaign as "Pre-Approved" and dates every page today, so its pages can't show whether a campaign is still running.
2. I ran web searches for the sites neither network matched, and to confirm the direct programs.

**Caveats:**
- The payouts are **"up to" headline rates**. They change often, and new and repeat customers are often paid different rates. Check the network dashboard before you promote a site.
- A dash (—) means no page was found under the store names I tried. It does not prove the network has no campaign.
- Abbreviations: **CPS** is per sale, **CPA** per action, **CPL** per lead, **CPI** per app install, **CPR** per registration, **CPC** per click.
